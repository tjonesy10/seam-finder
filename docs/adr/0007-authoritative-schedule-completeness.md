# ADR 0007: Independent schedule inventory and durable source-to-database completeness evidence

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-07) by owner merge of PR #20, commit `dc72cc544b575a35420dd1f550babcacb31a248f`, at 2026-11-07T01:10:55Z. Cosmo had approved the contract directly on 2026-11-06 19:14 EST: independent season-schedule verification, reviewed each season and when games change, fail closed without valid evidence. It refines F005's undefined “verified complete season schedule” prerequisite in Accepted ADR 0005 without editing ADR 0005 or ADR 0006 substance. It does not certify any season inventory, activate the schedule, approve production, or reopen the cadence or Sunday slate rule. Acceptance is the contract only. It is not acceptance of an implementation.

## Context

Premise rejected: **a fresh successful games load and internally valid rows establish that no required rows are missing**. A missing interior row or entire week preserves the first/last dates, all remaining row validity, and a fresh load receipt. At the earlier PR #19 head `81c43e8cf9619d04ef198772c4b2b23dfd35927a`, three strict-xfail counterexamples were recorded: interior Sunday deletion captured 2 salary rows, interior-week deletion captured 4, and an April run with a fresh but incomplete prior season incorrectly returned off_season. That historical result is why freshness of the remaining rows is not completeness. It is not the current test report.

nflreadr documents past/future schedule rows maintained by Lee Sharpe; its update schedule says five-minute updates. The schedules release says its formats update when the linked nfldata file changes. These establish intended publisher/update path, not an independent per-season complete game inventory. No independently published nflverse completeness manifest or per-season authoritative ID census was verified in the inspected documentation/release assets. CSV/parquet agreement is not independent: both mirror the same source. A local content hash establishes identity of received bytes, not completeness; a source version/timestamp establishes neither.

The NFL's 2026 announcement states 272 scheduled games and 17 per team; the same publication says final-week kickoff assignments are not initially known. The 2022 BUF–CIN cancellation demonstrates why 272 active/played rows, 17 appearances/team, and an assumed terminal week cannot be universal hard gates. Per-week 16-game assertions are also invalid because of byes. League publication/cancellation evidence is useful independent validation, not a newly authorized automated NFL feed.

## Decision

Recommend an **operator-approved independent season inventory**, plus a durable exact nflverse source artifact and source-to-PG verification receipt. The normal nflverse refresh can produce receipts automatically; manual review is required only for initial inventory onboarding and changes to identity/cancellation/coverage assertions, not each unchanged fetch or ordinary assigned-kickoff change. This is an explicit trust boundary, not a mathematical claim that an operator or upstream is infallible.

### 1. Authority inventory, independent of the ingested PG subset

- A designated operator prepares a bounded versioned JSON authority artifact from a **complete published league schedule reference**, comparing every season/REG matchup and its mapping to the corresponding nflverse game_id. Retain the reference bytes/URL, publication/retrieval evidence, SHA, reviewer identity, review time/method, and exact expected full REG game-id list/count. A script may prepare candidates, but cannot approve its own observed set as expected.
- Independently reconcile the league's explicitly published season-specific census and per-team matchup/appearance totals, with named, dated cancellation/addition/ID-change exceptions. For 2026 the published initial census is 272; do not infer it from a year range, an observed count, or week numbers. Census checks supplement **exact ID+matchup inventory equality**, not replace it. A 272-row replacement/substitution must fail too.
- An approved authority is selected by a separate trusted configuration SHA (`DK_SCHEDULE_AUTHORITY_SHA256`, or explicit operator-selected equivalent in a private configuration). Accepting an arbitrary manifest passed by the same capture process is not authority. The pinned root bundle enumerates the authorized per-season inventory SHA values and any finite gap-proof SHA values; child documents are not trusted merely because they are stored in Neon. A SHA without an explicit approval record is not a trust anchor. New seasons have no automatic approval.
- A manifest is not “NFL-signed.” League references are externally published material; the review assertion is ours. No official exact-ID manifest was acquired/certified in this investigation. Onboarding must actually provide and verify that full reference; the 272-game announcement alone is insufficient. Inability to verify it blocks onboarding, not a license to accept the raw input as complete.
- Cancellations are explicit tombstones retaining the original scheduled fixture and evidence. The authority declares exactly which IDs are represented in the current nflverse REG source and which scheduled fixtures are cancelled/removed. A missing fixture with no approved exception fails; null scores do not imply cancellation. Changes in ID/week/matchup or cancellation require a new independently reviewed authority revision. Rescheduling that preserves identity changes timing evidence, not inventory membership.

### 2. Durable raw upstream artifact and ingestion receipt

- Use the already-approved nflverse schedules release input (e.g. exact `games.parquet` bytes already fetched by the ordinary games loader). Store that full downloaded object privately before filtering, together with source URL, media type, SHA-256, byte length, source version/timestamp evidence, HTTP retrieval time/status/validators and a parser/schema version. Never serialize the surviving PG rows and call it raw upstream provenance.
- Within F005's approved private-migration gate, the normal games refresh emits a private control receipt binding raw artifact, authority revision, season, full REG source ID set/count, normalized schedule projection SHA and successful games load_run. This adds provenance/checking, not a new nflverse source or different upsert/delete rules. When private schema/gates are absent, ordinary F001/F003/F004 loading must continue without these optional receipts; F005 remains unavailable until they exist.
- Verify the independently expected IDs/matchups/census **before certifying** the upstream season slice. A valid HTTP response ending at a row boundary with a newly calculated SHA but missing a game is still rejected. Format footer/Content-Length/ETag checks are integrity/transport checks, not completeness proofs. If upstream and PG are both truncated, the independent inventory still differs.
- In the same source-season publication transaction, insert the normalized PG partition and durable receipt binding its full projection digest to the raw source. Public load_runs finalization may follow its existing separate transaction. Capture requires a succeeded matching load_run, receipt and exact current full-season PG equality in one consistent read, not merely a past receipt. Delete/insert/alter an interior row afterwards and verification fails even when the selected Sunday and endpoint dates appear plausible. Do not silently repair or bypass a mismatched PG partition during capture.
- The normalized projection includes all REG source IDs, season/type/week, teams, date/weekday and time including nulls. Source rows omitted due to explicit cancelled-fixture representation are handled only as authority specifies. Extra/substituted IDs, changed kickoffs/week/team values, missing rows and stale receipt associations fail until the normal refresh recertifies consistently.
- Proposed technical freshness default: actual retrieval at most 24 hours old at capture; retrieval cannot be in the future beyond 5 minutes clock skew. A recently copied old file is not a new retrieval. Preserve publisher last_updated separately: unchanged old publication time is not automatically stale if a real fresh retrieval validates the same object. No fabricated publication timestamp. A verified receipt requires a nonempty publisher version with retained evidence (for example the actual nflverse release timestamp or an independently bound nfldata commit); missing version evidence may be retained as rejected metadata, not certified. Finalized past-season closure evidence is immutable, but an active/upcoming decision needs fresh timing evidence. These are explicit engineering defaults for review, not guarantees against changes after retrieval.

### 3. Timing completeness is distinct from identity completeness

- Exact upcoming Sunday set comes from the certified complete source, using America/New_York instants, but only after every fixture that could intersect that Sunday has a resolved date/kickoff. A full inventory does not make a TBD time known.
- Unassigned late-season times must not disable otherwise verified earlier weeks. Each unresolved fixture must carry an evidence-backed possible-playing interval from the reviewed league schedule/flex notice. It may be excluded only if that entire interval is disjoint from the target Sunday. These are explicit publication-derived intervals, not automatically constructed week-1/week-18 or seven-day heuristics. Missing/unbounded/contradictory interval or one intersecting target Sunday → schedule_evidence_missing before any DK request. A fixture known to be on that Sunday but with unknown kickoff also fails: it might lie inside the 1:00–4:30 PM window.
- Assigned kickoffs may update automatically from fresh nflverse timing evidence when identity/approved interval constraints still hold; compare them against current PG and create a new receipt. A move outside a reviewed allowed interval or an identity/week change requires reviewed revision, not stretching the interval automatically. This is the tradeoff for explicit independent scope; expected flex windows can be reviewed initially to avoid weekly operator work.
- Boundaries use conservative earliest/latest **possible** REG instants across the full certified inventory, accounting for known postponements and explicit cancellations. An unresolved postponement with no safe bound prevents off-season classification; original kickoff dates are not proof it ended.
- No calendar-month, observed min/max, “week 18 present,” or incomplete prior-season fallback chooses the relevant season. A decision needs explicit reviewed calendar coverage: an authority's active-season coverage interval, or a league-supported interseason gap attestation. For a gap, require certified prior closure plus the next published season's verified earliest possible REG instant, or an independently documented league no-REG-through bound. The gap artifact lists all supporting authority revisions/periods and expires at its bound. Merely knowing last year's final game cannot declare an unlimited off-season.
- Therefore April with an incomplete prior inventory always fails. April with a complete prior inventory but no independently supported next-season/gap coverage also fails rather than guessing. This does not prevent verified in-season captures; positive off-season success is possible with a valid supported gap artifact. Cosmo approved this fail-closed coverage rule on 2026-11-06 19:14 EST. Approving the rule does not certify a season inventory.

### 4. Failure, audit, and scope

All missing/unapproved/truncated/stale/mismatched/ambiguous authority, raw artifact, receipt, PG or timing coverage yields `schedule_evidence_missing`, exit 1, zero DK calls and no usable salary snapshot. Record private subreasons/diffs/artifact references and existing monotonic attempt timing; public notes contain codes/counts only. Preserve last good salary data. Fully verified in-season but genuinely empty Sunday set is `slate_unavailable`, not off_season. A verified gap alone yields skipped/off_season without DK calls. The approved Wednesday cadence, exact Sunday match, F004 path, salary validity, denial stops, storage guards and source privacy do not weaken.

## Reasoning

The independent inventory detects missing rows regardless of where they disappeared. Raw hashes/receipts make that proof reproducible and catch post-ingestion PG damage. Identity review does not repeat on every ordinary kickoff refresh. Separating unknown future timing avoids requiring every final-week kickoff to be assigned in September. Explicit gap coverage prevents incomplete historical data from proving universal absence.

## Rejected alternatives and why

- Fresh load/version, valid observed rows, endpoints or auto week census: all three PR19 counterexamples refute them; they observe no independent expectation.
- Trust the direct full upstream download without independent inventory: fixes only PG omission, accepts silent upstream truncation.
- Require 272 rows / 17 games per team everywhere: misses exact identity substitution and rejects legitimate exceptions; wrong for other season formats. Useful only when season-specific published evidence supports that exact census.
- Cross-check CSV versus parquet or release versus nfldata: same maintained dataset/derivation, not independent authority.
- Automatically scrape NFL/ESPN as a second production feed: additional integration/source/error semantics and scope, unnecessary for this bounded fix; not authorized here.
- Accept a self-generated manifest/checksum/completeness flag: circular proof. Manifest approval must be external to capture and independently reviewed.
- Always fail every schedule forever: safe but fails the job. The proposed producer + seasonal authority supports normal automatic in-season positive tests and appropriately certified off-season gaps.

## Consequences

Cosmo accepted seasonal and change review, and fail-closed behavior when proof is missing, on 2026-11-06 19:14 EST. An operator must still onboard an actual full reference. Approving this text does not certify one. Implementation needs a small opt-in schedule provenance producer in the existing games refresh plus verification in F005. It does not add a production source crawler or automatic authority approval. Artifacts and receipts obey existing private storage guards and are retained without automatic purging. The three earlier strict-xfail counterexamples remain required ordinary regressions. Contract acceptance is not implementation acceptance. Production rollout remains unapproved.
